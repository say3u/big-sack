Big Sack - Changes from my One-Pager
David Nguyen

The main idea is still the same as my one-pager. You're tied to a heavy sack with a rope, you
hold Shift to brace, swing it with A and D, and jump when it swings up to launch yourself. Most
of what changed is the setting, the level, and the stuff around the main mechanic.

Setting / Story
My one-pager was a porter climbing floating sky islands with a sack that had an iron ball in it.
I actually built the sky tower first, but there wasn't really a reason to climb it. So I changed
it to a sewer cleaner climbing up into a clogged toilet with a heavy tool bag. The poop platforms
are the clog, and you win by flushing it. It gives the climb a goal and the flush is a way more
fun ending.

Level
I still have 3 sections, but now they're The Tunnels (tutorial with hints), The Pipe, and The
Bowl. The bowl gets harder as you go up. The bottom half shows you one new thing at a time and
the top half mixes them together. I also added moving poops and rubber ducks that bounce you,
which weren't in my one-pager. The crumbling islands turned into "worried poops" that crumble
after 3 seconds and then come back.

Falling
In my one-pager falling just dropped you to a lower island. That was way too harsh in the bowl
since you'd fall all the way down the pipe. So I added checkpoint flags. If you fall too far you
go back to your last flag, and R or the pause menu sends you back if you get stuck.

Mechanics
- The launch works how I planned (the sack's speed times a multiplier gets added to your jump),
  but I had to cap it because people were launching way too high.
- The sack hops up with you when you jump so the rope doesn't yank you back down.
- I tried making the sack swing all the way over your head but it felt confusing, so I cut it.
- I was worried about the sack getting stuck on platforms, so now it can go through them from
  below but still land on top.

Art
I used Kenney assets for the tiles, flags, and fonts, plus pixel art for the cleaner, tool bag,
poops, duck, toilet, and buttons. The player only has idle and walk frames instead of all the
poses I planned, and the background is the inside of the toilet instead of the sky.

Sound
Still no background music like I planned. I took out the rope creak because it was annoying and
squeaky, and the sack sounds were distracting. I added tile footsteps, a whoosh for launches, a
slide whistle for big falls, a boing for ducks, a squish and plop for crumbling poops, and a
flush at the end. I also added a volume slider.

Extras I added
Title screen, how to play screen, pause menu, tutorial hints, section banners, and a win screen
that shows your time, falls, and best time.

AI Use
The game idea, the one-pager, the toilet theme, and all the playtesting and design calls were
mine, like cutting the overhead swing, capping the launch, and redoing the bowl when it didn't
flow. I used Claude to help write and debug the code, set a couple things up in Unity, draw
the duck, make the sounds, and do the Git commits.
